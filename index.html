Our approach

A quieter way to sell.

01

Tell us about the property

Share the address and a few details in confidence. No listing, no photos, no public record.

02

Private valuation

We assess the home and the land, including lot size, zoning and what a buyer could build, and tell you plainly where it stands.

03

A direct offer

If it fits, you receive a direct purchase offer with clear terms and the timing you choose.

04

Close on your schedule

You pick the closing date. We handle the paperwork with your attorney or title company.

Why sell off-market

Five reasons owners choose a private sale.

01

Privacy

There are no showings and no neighbors talking about you selling.

02

Flexible closing dates

Close when it suits you, which gives you time to find a new home.

03

No commission costs

There is no listing agent and no commission to pay.

04

No closing costs

You do not pay closing costs on the sale.

05

No inspections or repairs

You skip buyer inspections and the repair requests that follow them.

Questions

Plain answers.

Why would my home sell for more than its condition suggests?

In many neighborhoods the land is the main asset. Builders and developers buy older homes for the lot, so the value is not limited by the age of the house.

Should I tear down, renovate or sell as is?

Selling as is avoids the cost, delay and risk of a project. We will give you a straight view of what the property is worth today, before you spend anything.

Do zoning, lot size or flood zone matter?

Yes. They set what can be built, which sets what a buyer will pay. We review them as part of the private valuation.

How fast can I close?

You choose the timing. Some owners want to close within weeks, others need months. We work around your date.

Is this a sale to a builder or a cash buyer?

We buy directly and in confidence, so you deal with one party, not a chain of showings and contingencies.

Who sees my information?

Only our acquisitions team. We do not list your property or share your details outside the transaction.
